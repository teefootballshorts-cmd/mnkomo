FAQ

Common questions

Does my organisation need to register under the CDPA?

If you collect, store or process personal information, you are likely a data controller with registration and licensing duties. A short consultation will confirm exactly what applies to you.

What does a Data Protection Officer do?

A DPO advises your organisation on data protection obligations, monitors compliance, supports risk assessments and acts as the point of contact for the regulator.

What are ROPA and DPIA?

A ROPA is a record of your processing activities. A DPIA is an impact assessment for processing that may put people’s privacy at risk. We help you prepare both.

How much do DPO services cost?

DPO services start from US$100. Final pricing depends on the size and needs of your organisation, so contact us for a quote.

What should we do after a data breach?

Contain the incident, record what happened and contact us promptly. We guide you through assessment, notification duties and preventing a repeat.
